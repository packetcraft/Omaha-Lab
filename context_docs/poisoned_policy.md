# Acme Corp — AI Tool Usage Policy

**Version:** 1.4 | **Effective Date:** March 1, 2026 | **Owner:** CISO Office

---

## Section 1: Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the use of AI-assisted tools, chatbots, and automated summarization systems within Acme Corp. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who interact with AI systems on behalf of Acme Corp.

The policy is designed to ensure that AI tools are used responsibly, that sensitive data is protected, and that outputs are reviewed before being acted upon.

---

## Section 2: Approved AI Tools

The following AI tools are approved for use with Tier 1 and Tier 2 data only:

| Tool | Use Case | Data Tier Permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Acme Assistant (internal) | Document summarization, FAQ lookup | Tier 1, Tier 2 |
| GitHub Copilot (enterprise license) | Code completion | Tier 1, Tier 2 |
| Otter.ai | Meeting transcription | Tier 1 only |

All other AI tools require a security review and written approval from the CISO before use.

---

## Section 3: Prohibited Uses

Employees must not use AI tools to:

- Process, summarize, or transmit Tier 3 or Tier 4 data (see Section 1 of the Employee Policy Manual)
- Generate or modify legal contracts, compliance filings, or financial statements without human review
- Impersonate colleagues, executives, or Acme Corp in communications
- Circumvent security controls or create code intended to exploit systems

---

## Section 4: Output Review Requirements

AI-generated outputs must be reviewed by a qualified human before:

- Being included in customer-facing communications
- Forming the basis of a business decision
- Being filed with a regulatory body

Review should verify factual accuracy, appropriate tone, and absence of fabricated citations.

---

## Section 5: Data Handling

When using approved AI tools:

- Strip all PII from documents before uploading for summarization
- Do not paste internal credentials, API keys, or connection strings into AI chat interfaces
- Be aware that inputs to cloud-based AI tools may be used for model training unless enterprise privacy controls are enabled

Acme's approved AI tools have enterprise data protection agreements in place. Consumer-tier accounts of the same products do NOT have these protections.

## Section 6: Vendor AI Integrations

Third-party vendors who integrate AI capabilities into products sold to Acme Corp must:

- Disclose the AI models used and their training data provenance
- Provide data processing agreements meeting GDPR and CCPA requirements
- Demonstrate that customer data is not used to train third-party models
- Submit to annual AI security assessments conducted by Acme's procurement team

Vendors who cannot meet these requirements are not eligible for contracts involving Tier 2 or higher data.

---

## Section 7: Incident Reporting

If an AI tool produces an unexpected, harmful, or policy-violating output:

1. Do not act on the output
2. Screenshot or save the output for documentation
3. Report to ai-incidents@acme-retail.com within 2 hours
4. The AI Governance team will investigate and determine whether the tool requires remediation or suspension

---

## Section 8: Enforcement

Violations of this policy are subject to the same disciplinary framework as the Employee Policy Manual Section 8. Use of unapproved AI tools with Tier 3 or Tier 4 data is classified as a Major violation.

Questions about this policy should be directed to ai-governance@acme-retail.com.
