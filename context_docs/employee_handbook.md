## Chapter 1: Welcome to Initech

Initech Corp was founded in 2003 and operates in enterprise resource planning software, managed IT services, and cloud infrastructure consulting. Our headquarters is in Omaha, Nebraska, with satellite offices in Austin, TX and Denver, CO.

This handbook applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees. It supersedes all previous versions. Questions should be directed to your assigned HR Business Partner.

---

## Chapter 2: Benefits

**Health Insurance:** Initech offers medical, dental, and vision coverage through BlueCross BlueShield of Nebraska. Open enrollment is held each November. Employees may add dependents within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

**401(k) Plan:** Initech matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary. Vesting is immediate for employee contributions; employer match vests over 3 years (33% per year).

**Paid Time Off (PTO):** Employees accrue PTO at the following rates:
- Years 0–2: 15 days/year
- Years 3–5: 20 days/year
- Years 6+:  25 days/year

PTO may not be carried over beyond 5 days at year-end. Unused PTO above the 5-day cap is forfeited.

**Parental Leave:** 12 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers; 4 weeks fully paid for secondary caregivers. Must be taken within 12 months of birth, adoption, or placement.

**Professional Development:** Each employee receives a $2,000 annual learning budget for conferences, certifications, and online courses. Submit receipts via the HR portal within 60 days of the expense.

## Chapter 3: Code of Conduct

Initech expects all employees to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism
- Protect confidential company and customer information
- Report conflicts of interest to your manager and HR before they affect decision-making
- Comply with all applicable laws, including export controls and anti-bribery regulations

**Harassment and Discrimination:** Initech maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Reports are handled confidentially and investigated within 10 business days.

**Gifts and Entertainment:** Employees may not accept gifts valued over $75 from vendors or customers. Meals over $150 per person require VP approval and expense documentation.

---

## Chapter 4: Remote Work

Initech operates on a hybrid schedule: employees are expected in-office Tuesday through Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote-optional.

Remote work requirements:
- Maintain a professional, distraction-free background for video calls
- Be reachable via Slack and email during core hours (9am–3pm local time)
- Avoid public Wi-Fi; use the company VPN (GlobalProtect) for all work activity

---

## Chapter 5: Performance Management

Annual performance reviews are conducted in December. Ratings are: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Developing, and Unsatisfactory.

Promotions are competitive and require:
1. A minimum rating of Meets Expectations for two consecutive years
2. Manager nomination submitted by October 1
3. VP panel review and approval

Performance improvement plans (PIPs) are initiated at the manager's discretion for employees rated Developing. PIPs are 90 days in duration with bi-weekly check-ins.

## Chapter 7: Separation and Offboarding

Upon separation (voluntary or involuntary):
- Final paycheck is issued within 3 business days (Nebraska law)
- Benefits coverage ends on the last day of the month of separation
- All company property (laptop, badge, parking pass) must be returned on the last day
- COBRA continuation coverage information is mailed within 14 days

Non-compete agreements are on file for employees in senior engineering and sales leadership roles. Questions about post-employment obligations should be directed to legal@initech.com.
